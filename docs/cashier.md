# Cashier opening and closing

When closed, the highlighted header control opens the opening popup on desktop, mobile and POS. When open, it opens `/cashier` to review and close. Accounts → Daily Closing opens the same screen. Each shop has its own daily session; a previous open day must be closed before starting today. Cashiers can open and close repeatedly on the same day through the opening popup, without a cycle limit or a separate reopening permission. Each opening/reopening and closing records its counted cash, washing assignment/rate, actor and withdrawal details in Opening & Closing History. Day totals remain cumulative, so earlier receipts and withdrawals are not repeated. The legacy reason-based reopen endpoint still requires `account.reopen_closing`.

While closed, the server blocks job card creation, quick jobs, job billing and parts sales, including direct POST/JSON requests. Bike intake and viewing existing jobs remain available. Existing receipt collection workflows remain available.

Opening defaults to the total physical cash carried forward in Main, Washing and Spare Parts. Bank funds and non-cash receipts are excluded. The popup autofills editable Main and Spare Parts opening cash amounts, plus any carried washing cash. The opening total is read-only and updates automatically from these account amounts. Each account amount must be nonnegative; the server also validates that their sum matches the total. Changing the account amounts requires a reason and posts separate account corrections; the difference is never automatically charged entirely to Main. Reopening changes current cash through dated count corrections while preserving the original day opening. Cash account opening balances are frozen for the session, and verified closing cash carries into the following day.

Settled payments collect Service → Labor → Washing → Other → Spare Parts, consuming each charge before the next. Main receives Service, Labor and Other. The existing full-invoice discount/tax account budgets are preserved. Unpaid bills, held cheques and salary deductions do not count as received money. A cheque takes its priority share when it clears. Split invoice allocations sharing one receipt are counted once. Parts-only sales, including tax, belong to Spare Parts. The migration corrects receipts in the currently open period (or today); it leaves earlier closed periods unchanged.

The cashier cash totals show physical cash only: opening, actual cash collections, other cash movements, expenses and cash in hand. The closing form requires one nonnegative physical closing balance after owner withdrawal; Main Account and Spare Parts closing amounts auto-fill and can be edited. Main includes the washing cash settlement; Spare Parts deducts owner withdrawal live while preserving a manual correction. Their sum must match the physical count. Changes require a reason and reconcile each edited account separately. Older single-count API submissions remain supported, posting their variance to Main. Washing cash is transferred into Main at closing, leaving its cash balance zero. The transfer is recorded in both ledgers and history, excludes bank allocations, and never counts as another collection or cash expense. Bank Account also auto-fills as an editable closing field. A change requires a reason and posts only to the bank ledger as a non-cash reconciliation. Bank is excluded from the counted cash, owner withdrawal limits and cash carry-forward; its verified balance is saved separately in history and PDF/Excel exports. Owner withdrawal is a cash drawing from available Spare Parts cash and updates that balance and the total live. Previously saved withdrawals are never repeated on reopening. Count differences require a reason and create cash reconciliation entries.

The highlighted All Collections header button opens a separate window. It shows settled cash and bank receipts once, with date, text and All / Cash only / Bank / Bank transfers filters and a Service, Labor, Washing, Other and Spare Parts breakdown. Account transfers and unpaid bills are not customer collections.

Opening, closing and protected sales serialize through the system account row locks. Closing checks a version of the displayed balances and ledger entries; a stale form refreshes the account inputs instead of silently overwriting newer movements. Closed sessions retain their verified balances and summaries. New daily closing records and PDF/Excel exports show the cash wallets and a separate Bank Account (Non-cash) row, separately for each shop. Older closing records retain their original account snapshots.

Deploy with the normal application migration and asset build steps: `php artisan migrate --force`, `npm ci`, `npm run build`. The schema migration adds cashier sessions, separates daily closing dates by shop and adds Washing fields without deleting existing financial records. Rollback to the old globally unique closing date requires resolving any dates that have closings in both shops first.

Tests: `php -d memory_limit=1G vendor/bin/pest --compact tests/Feature/CashierSessionTest.php`; `node --test tests/Unit/cashier.test.mjs`.

## Washing assignment at opening

The popup suggests carried cash on first opening and current cash in hand on a same-day reopening. Any difference needs a reason and posts only that difference. It selects today’s active washing staff member and a fixed commission initially defaulting to Rs. 60 per job containing washing. The checkbox saves a new fixed default separately for Workshop and Helmet shop. Without the checkbox, an override applies to that opening only. Assign later keeps washing unassigned. Zero commission is permitted; a job with no washing charge earns no washing commission.

Normal jobs and quick jobs inherit the active cashier’s person and fixed amount, with editable overrides on each job. Billing creates one pending Washing commission for that amount, regardless of the washing charge. Changing a washing charge does not scale the fixed commission. It appears in staff commissions and salary previews, separately from Service + Labor commission. Paid or payroll-linked records remain protected.

Migration `2026_10_29_000005_use_fixed_washing_commission` adds fixed amount fields, updates eligible pending washing commissions to Rs. 60, and preserves paid/payroll-linked earnings and their historical percentage records. Historical opening events retain their original commission snapshot. Future opening/closing events record the fixed amount. No account transfers occur during migration; washing cash settlement happens only when the cashier is closed.
