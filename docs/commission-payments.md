# Separate technician commission

Commission is paid from Staff → Commission. Salary calculations no longer add pending job, parts or washing commission, and salary finalization no longer consumes or marks these commissions paid. Newly generated/refreshed salary slips omit the commission earnings row. Historical finalized/paid salary totals stay unchanged. A legacy draft containing commission must be refreshed before finalizing. Reopening an earlier unpaid finalized salary still releases its previously linked commission; refreshing the draft then removes it from salary.

Choose Daily, Monthly, Custom dates or All dates and optionally a person. The page groups earned commission, deductions, separate payments, earlier salary/settlement coverage and remaining balance by person. **View / Deduct / Pay** opens a popup with invoice/job references, bike numbers, type, per-bill amounts and settlement history. Print / Save Commission Slip produces a separate person-specific statement for the same earned-date period. Payments are shown as allocated against that period's bills, including the appropriate portion of a payment spanning multiple days.

A deduction requires a description, reduces commission payable and appears on the commission slip. It does not reduce salary or record a cash expense. Full and partial payments are allocated oldest bill first, cannot exceed the available balance, and create a commission payment header and bill allocations. Cash payments post one expense; a non-cash payment from a logical account posts to that account and Bank, without becoming a cash receipt. Bank-only payments post once. Bank Account cannot be paired with Cash. Payment dates cannot be future or precede the commission being settled.

Forms carry a fingerprint of all commission amounts/statuses in the selected period. Settlements lock the staff and commission rows, reject stale/repeated submissions, and audit each allocation. Commission already linked to salary, previously settled, cancelled or from another shop cannot be paid again. Deductions/partial payouts protect job commission bases and washing assignments from silent rewriting. Commission settlement does not settle customer invoices, change salary balances, or duplicate salary advances/repair deductions.

Migration `2026_10_29_000008_add_separate_commission_settlements` adds payment/deduction counters, payment headers, allocations, deduction descriptions and commission shop ownership. Existing invoice-linked commissions inherit the invoice shop; historical records without an invoice default to Workshop. Financial amounts and old salary links are not rewritten. Apply the migration before deploying this code. Refresh existing salary drafts to remove automatic commission before finalization.

Implementation and verification use local development and the isolated testing database only. No live server or live financial data was changed.
